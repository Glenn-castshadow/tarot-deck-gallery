# Ishtar Insights — Arts and Crafts deck study

Glenn requested an Arts and Crafts inspired deck, possibly with a Freemason vibe. Developed through The Fool, The Magician, and The High Priestess, the direction now extends to a complete 78-card deck and matching back; see [production notes](../COMPANION-DECKS.md).

## Visual language

William Morris botanical order and Walter Crane allegorical illustration inform the direction: precise woodcut-like contours, selective hatching, flat mineral colors and matte paper texture. Forest green, muted indigo, madder red, ochre and warm ivory form the palette. Acanthus, oak, acacia and restrained blossoms organize the frame. Central figures and tarot symbols should remain readable at card size.

The Masonic influence is an interpretive visual reference: paired pillars, a measured threshold, craft tools, square and compasses, architectural order, sun and moon. The deck is not presented as an official Masonic publication or an account of Masonic ritual.

## Three initial subjects

- The Fool: a traveler and dog at a garden gateway, suggesting an opening into learning.
- The Magician: a craftsperson at a workbench, with the four tarot suit objects and measuring tools.
- The High Priestess: a seated reader, scroll and veil between two pillars.

## Files and production status

`proofs/` contains the initial unlettered studies; `prompts.json` records their prompts. `generation-plan.json` records the complete selected deck and exact prompts. Created with the built-in image generation tool. Native sources are approximately 971 × 1619 px; complete typeset canvases use 70 × 120 mm trim, 3 mm bleed and 600 PPI output. The manifest records native resolution separately.
