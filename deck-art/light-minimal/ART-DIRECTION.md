# Ishtar Insights — light deck study

Glenn requested a Moebius-inspired deck that is light and minimal. The first three studies explored The Fool, The Magician, and The High Priestess. The direction now extends to a complete 78-card deck and matching back; see [production notes](../COMPANION-DECKS.md).

## Accepted direction and latest feedback

The user described the first studies as being in the right vein but too desaturated. Preserve their fine line drawing, spacious surreal landscapes, spare compositions, and open sky. Strengthen the color: clear blue, dusty coral, iris-lavender, ochre and warm ivory. Airy should not mean washed out.

`proofs/v1/` holds the first pale studies. `proofs/v2/` holds the color revisions, with subjects and compositions preserved. All are unlettered illustrations; final card titles and numbers belong in a consistent production layer.

## Extending the deck

- Major Arcana: one clear figure or symbolic event, with room for quiet landscape.
- Wands: coral and ochre accents; craft, movement, open land and fire.
- Cups: blue and warm ivory; water, vessels, relationships and reflection.
- Swords: blue-gray and lavender; air, edges, thought and perspective.
- Pentacles: golden ochre and earth tones; work, gardens, material life and stewardship.

Use recognizable scenes for the numbered Minor Arcana rather than relying only on the number of suit objects. Keep faces, hands, and suit symbols readable at card size.

## Files and production status

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. `prompts.json` records the initial studies; `generation-plan.json` records the complete selected deck and exact prompts. Native sources are approximately 971 × 1619 px. The complete typeset canvases use 70 × 120 mm trim, 3 mm bleed and 600 PPI output; native source detail is recorded separately in `manifest.csv`.
