# Ishtar Insights — print specification

This deck is being prepared for direct printing on a flat sheet and then cutting down, rather than for pre-made card blanks.

## Working format

- Finished card: **70 × 120 mm** (standard tarot proportion)
- Bleed: **3 mm on every edge**
- Working artboard: **76 × 126 mm**
- Production raster: **600 PPI**, approximately **1795 × 2976 px** per card
- EufyMake E1 bed reference: **330 × 420 mm**; a sheet layout will be generated after the card fronts and backs are approved
- Safe area: keep titles, numbers, and key symbols at least 4 mm inside the trim line
- Export: individual PNG fronts, one mirrored back, and cut-ready sheet layouts

The source illustrations are generated without lettering. Card names, Roman numerals, suit labels, and any guide text will be typeset in the production layer so the E1 receives clean, repeatable typography.

## Visual direction

Ishtar Insights uses deep plum and midnight indigo with luminous cyan, violet, magenta, aqua, cream, and warm gold. The recurring visual language is a lotus outline, celestial geometry, reflective water, ancient Mesopotamian atmosphere, and holographic foil-like light. Every card should have a distinct scene while sharing the same ornamental system and generous trim-safe framing.
