# Built-in image tool pilot — 2026-09-08

Four fresh trials: two repetitions each of dark Seven of Swords and Arts and Crafts Nine of Wands. This was a workflow pilot using the built-in image tool; its underlying model version was undisclosed. It is not a comparison between models or a result for the full seven-case suite.

| Case | First pass | After one permitted correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Seven of Swords | 2/2 | 2/2 (no corrections needed) |
| Arts and Crafts Nine of Wands | 1/2 | 1/2 |
| Total | 3/4 | 3/4 |

Codex performed and recorded the visual reviews. Both sword images showed five carried swords and two planted swords. One wand image showed eight fence wands plus one held; the other showed only six fence wands plus one held. A targeted edit requesting the two missing fence wands left the visible count at seven. The loop retained both failed attempts and stopped at its two-attempt limit.

All five native images passed file, size, ratio and duplicate checks. Native dimensions were 971 pixels wide by approximately 1619–1620 high. Technical success alone therefore did not establish a correct tarot card. The sum of observed tool-call latencies was 432 seconds, including queuing during parallel calls; this is not wall-clock elapsed time or an isolated provider-speed measurement. Dollar cost was unavailable.

The main finding is a concrete count/edit failure despite strong style adherence. Four trials are too few to estimate general reliability or select a best model. Compare additional candidates on the same frozen full suite before making that decision. These results have not replaced any production deck artwork.
