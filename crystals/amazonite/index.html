What Amazonite means

Amazonite carries the blue-green of shallow water over sand, and it loosens words that get stuck between the heart and the mouth. Keep it close when a conversation matters and you are afraid of saying it wrong.

Emotional properties

A hard phone call waits on the screen, the cursor blinking over a message rewritten three times already. Amazonite loosens the knot that confrontation ties in the throat before it ever reaches the mouth, so the plain version of what you mean has an easier way out. Wearing it during that call, or the letter you keep putting off, steadies the shake in your voice. The true thing, said first, rarely needs the apology that usually follows it.

Spiritual properties

Amazonite gives courage of the quiet kind, the sort that shows up as a decision to speak plainly rather than a dramatic stand. Working with it in meditation asks where you soften the truth just to keep the peace. Heart and throat chakras meet in its colour, feeling and expression moving as one current instead of two. Let the two stop working against each other.

Physical properties

Amazonite feels cool and faintly waxy against the skin, softer than quartz and quicker to warm in a closed hand. Screen time and long calls tighten the jaw and shoulders without you noticing, and its coolness against the throat is a plain cue to unclench. The shoulders drop first, then the grip on the phone, then whatever you were bracing for. Let that be enough for now.

Care and cleansing

Amazonite owes its blue-green to traces of lead, so keep it out of elixirs, wipe rather than soak it, and wash your hands after handling. Charge it in moonlight, since strong sun can bleach it. Store it wrapped, because a knock can split it cleanly along its grain.

A question to sit with

What truth have you been softening just to keep things comfortable?
