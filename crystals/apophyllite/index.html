What Apophyllite means

Apophyllite grows in pyramid-shaped clusters so clear they look like they are made of water and light rather than stone. Its name means 'to leaf away', from the way it flakes apart in a flame, and it asks you to let old layers go just as easily.

Emotional properties

Hold apophyllite up to a lamp and it pulls the curtains open on a mind crowded with old worry, without forcing anything. More light reaches feelings that had been sitting in shadow for a while, not as a flood, just a widening crack. A lightness follows, not euphoria, just less weight carried into the next hour. That small shift is frequently where real change begins.

Spiritual properties

Apophyllite's pyramid form and near-transparency make it suited to meditation, built for looking straight through rather than at. Dreams and quiet sitting alike tend to sharpen when it sits nearby, inviting you to notice the noticing itself rather than chase a particular vision. It asks for stillness more than effort, patience more than technique. A clear stone rewards a clear question.

Physical properties

Apophyllite holds water locked inside its crystal structure, and it feels cool and glassy in the hand. Work with it for slow, unforced breathing when stress shortens your breath. It splits along flat planes and its points chip, so hold it lightly in an open palm.

Care and cleansing

Apophyllite can flake if it meets sudden heat, so keep it out of direct sun and away from anything hot. A gentle rinse in cool water is fine, but dry it soon after. Recharge it with a bell or singing bowl, and store it where its points cannot catch on anything.
