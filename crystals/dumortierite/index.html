What Dumortierite means

Dumortierite grows as fine blue-violet needles packed so densely together that the stone reads as one solid colour rather than a cluster of crystals. It steadies a mind that has too many threads running at once, one strand at a time.

Emotional properties

The fourth hour of revising for an exam is where enthusiasm gives out and discipline has to take over. Dumortierite's denim blue belongs to that hour: not exciting, just there, like a colleague who keeps working without comment. Discipline built this way outlasts any single burst of motivation.

Spiritual properties

Dumortierite has long stood in for costlier blue stones in jewellery, close enough in colour to pass at a glance yet entirely its own mineral underneath. Working with it asks where you have let an easier substitute stand in for the real, patient effort a goal actually needs. It is a throat stone as much as a third-eye one, so an insight is not finished until you have said it to someone.

Physical properties

Dumortierite feels dense and faintly grainy against the thumb, its packed needles giving it a texture unlike any polished gem. Rest it beside the notebook, and between pages lay your writing hand flat over it until the fingers uncurl from the shape of the pen. A hand that has held one grip all afternoon needs a different texture as much as it needs a break.

Care and cleansing

A quick rinse under running water leaves dumortierite unmarked, since it is a hard and durable stone. Charge it in morning sunlight, which its deep blue takes well. Wrapped in a scrap of linen, it will not mark the softer stones it shares a drawer with.

A question to sit with

Which hour of your work would you skip if you could, and what does staying for it teach you?
