What Tourmalinated quartz means

Black needles of tourmaline crossed this quartz while it was still growing, and the clear stone simply closed around them instead of pushing them out. What could have fractured the crystal became part of its structure instead.

Emotional properties

Black strokes cross the clear stone at every angle, like pencil marks on a window, and the window still lets the light through. It suits a family gathering or a heated group chat, anywhere other people's strong opinions pile up around you. The dark lines are what you heard; the clear quartz is you, still seeing straight through them.

Spiritual properties

Rest your gaze on one black needle until the clear quartz around it softens out of focus, then look into the clear and watch the needle blur instead. Neither can be seen without the other behind it. Temperance, the card it partners, pours water between two cups without spilling, and this stone asks for the same easy traffic between your dark moods and your light ones.

Physical properties

Turn a piece of tourmalinated quartz to the light and the black threads seem to hover somewhere inside rather than on the surface, an odd, layered depth to look at. Set it at the front of the mat before a balancing pose, tree or eagle, where the feet have to root while the head stays level. Its black and its clear hold still together, and that is the whole trick of balance.

Care and cleansing

Like any clear quartz, a polished sphere of tourmalinated quartz can focus sunlight into a flame, so give it a shaded shelf, well back from any window. Incense smoke cleanses it, the ring of a bell charges it, and a cloth cover keeps dust and sun off it between uses.
