What Smoky quartz means

Trace aluminium and long exposure to the earth's own background radiation turn clear quartz the brown-grey of smoky quartz, a colouring the trade now often speeds up with a lab irradiator. Scottish jewellers once called it cairngorm, after the mountains where it was gathered for kilt pins and sgian-dubh handles.

Emotional properties

With ten tabs open and a fourth notification arriving, keep smoky quartz beside the keyboard, where the eye lands between screens. Of everything on the desk, only the stone will not blink, flash or ask to be answered. Each time a new pull arrives, look at the stone first and the screen second.

Spiritual properties

Once a meditation or an evening with the cards is over, the mind can stay up in the air for a while. Smoky quartz returns you to the room: hold it for the last minute of any session, and feel the chair and your own feet come back. A practice that ends on the ground is easier to pick up again tomorrow.

Physical properties

Smoky quartz comes into its own on days spent standing: a market stall, a day behind a shop counter, a queue that will not move. Drop a tumbled piece into a coat pocket, and whenever you shift your weight, press a thumb against it and feel both heels meet the floor. On days like that, a root stone matters more to the legs than to the head.

Care and cleansing

Strong heat bleaches smoky quartz, and long sun can pale some pieces; a clear sphere can act as a lens and scorch a curtain, so keep it in shade and away from flame. Water does quartz no harm, so scrub it with soapy water and charge it in cedar smoke. It lives best in a drawer, not on a shelf.
