Two of Pentacles

balance · adaptability · priorities

What Two of Pentacles means

On Friday the only figure that counts is the one that can be produced. A person can be owed more than they owe and still be unable to pay, because solvency and liquidity are separate properties and the Two names the distance between them: the money is real, the timing is not arranged. Figures that ought to describe the same holding come apart because they are separated in time. Jupiter, which enlarges, takes the first face of Capricorn, a sign ruled by the planet of deadlines, and enlargement is no use whatever against a date. The question the Two puts is when, not how much, and a sum does not state that about itself.

Upright

Several demands may need a flexible rhythm. Decide which priorities deserve a steady place and which can change as your circumstances do.

Reversed

Juggling can conceal that there is simply too much to hold. Reduce one demand or renegotiate a commitment instead of asking yourself to balance indefinitely.

A question to sit with

What needs a real priority instead of a place in the juggling?

Traditional attribution

Jupiter in Capricorn. Attributions follow the Golden Dawn with Waite's numbering.
