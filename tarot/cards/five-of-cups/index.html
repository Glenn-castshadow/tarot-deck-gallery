Five of Cups

loss · disappointment · remaining support

What Five of Cups means

Mars rules Scorpio, which makes this face of it that placement doing precisely what it is equipped for: a subtraction performed cleanly, with no error in it to appeal against. The Five’s subject is what goes on working afterwards — an attachment that does not dissolve when its object does, still operating with nothing left to operate on. Grief is the name for that residue, and its size is a measurement of the worth of what is gone. The Five sets out the stretch of time in which a person goes on being attached to something that has stopped being there.

Upright

Let disappointment have its rightful place. When you are ready, turn gently toward what remains available; acknowledging support does not erase what mattered.

Reversed

A loss may be finding a place in a larger story. Allow repair or reconnection at your own pace, without requiring yourself to be finished with grief.

A question to sit with

What remains worthy of care alongside what I have lost?

Traditional attribution

Mars in Scorpio. Attributions follow the Golden Dawn with Waite's numbering.
