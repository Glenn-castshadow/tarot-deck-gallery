# Mansions of the Moon on /sky/ (short design)

**Intent.** Glenn asked to "add lunar mansions to the site", the second item on the 2026-11-14 research
shortlist: cheap arithmetic on a Moon position the site already computes, with a strong lineage.

**Approved 2026-11-14** (Glenn: "Yes, build it") as proposed.

## What it is

A fourth section on `/sky/`, after planetary hours: the mansion the Moon is in now (number, name, span,
when it entered, when it leaves), the mansion of a saved birth Moon, and a list of all twenty-eight with
a short note on what tradition assigned to each.

## Approaches considered

1. **Own section, Agrippa's equal division from tropical 0° Aries** (chosen).
2. A line inside the sky calendar's "Moon now" tab. Rejected: no room for the list of twenty-eight.
3. Star-based (sidereal) mansions. Rejected for now: needs an ayanamsa choice the Western sections do not
   otherwise make, and would look like a second copy of the nakshatras. The page states the difference.

## Constraints, and where each comes from

- Tropical division from 0° Aries: Claude's judgement, shown to Glenn in the proposal and approved.
- The uses are reported as history, harsh ones included, with no advice: Claude's judgement, approved in
  the same proposal.
- Text checked against the public-domain 1651 translation and summarised in original words, not quoted:
  Claude's judgement, following the research note that the online edition's markup is rights-reserved.
- No new dependency and no browser storage: nothing here needs either.

## Left out

A date picker, the mansions in the month grid, talismanic images, and star-based boundaries.
