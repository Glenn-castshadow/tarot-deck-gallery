# Lots, firdaria and zodiacal releasing (design)

**Intent.** Glenn asked to "add zodiacal releasing, firdaria and the lots to the site", the third item on the
2026-11-14 research shortlist: it completes the traditional suite beside profections and horary, and is
arithmetic on a chart the site already calculates.

**Approved 2026-11-14** (Glenn: "Yes, build it") with the four picks in the table below. Three changes were
made after the source check and are marked.

## What it is

Nested at the end of the natal report's "Traditional techniques" section on `/charts/`: a table of the seven
Hermetic lots; firdaria with the current period and sub-period named and the full sequence in a collapsed list;
zodiacal releasing from Spirit and from Fortune to two levels, with peaks and the loosing of the bond flagged.

## Approaches considered

1. **Inside the existing Traditional techniques section** (chosen). The report already has the chart, the sect
   and the audience; nothing else on the site changes.
2. A new top-level section on `/charts/`. Rejected: it would need its own copy of the chart and a seventh fold.
3. A tab in "Chart in time". Rejected: that section is about returns and progressions, which move the chart;
   these read periods off the fixed natal chart.

(nothing here needs one); nothing stored.

## Left out

Releasing levels 3 and 4, releasing from Eros, the Valens and Firmicus lot variants, the after-Mars firdaria
variant, and a way to pick a date other than today.
