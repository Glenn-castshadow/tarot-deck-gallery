I Ching · Withdrawal

Hexagram 33: Dùn 遯

A mountain beneath open sky

Trigrams
Mountain below, Heaven above
Keyword
Withdrawal

What hexagram 33 means

Heaven draws away above a Mountain that stays fixed in place, the distance between them widening. A timely, deliberate withdrawal can protect what matters more than contesting ground that is already shifting.

A question to sit with

What would a timely, deliberate withdrawal protect right now?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

At the tail end of a retreat, notice that the last to leave stands nearest to what is being left. Where a withdrawal is already underway, this is a place to keep still and avoid starting anything new.

Line 2 · The inner position

An image of something bound fast with yellow oxhide describes a commitment that holds while others pull away. If a purpose is worth keeping, hold it quietly and firmly rather than arguing for it.

Line 3 · The threshold

Ties to people who depend on you can slow a withdrawal that ought to be clean. Notice which obligations are truly yours to carry, and tend them without letting them decide the whole direction.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Stepping back by choice, before anyone pushes, keeps your dignity and your options intact. The inner steadiness this asks for is easier to find when you are clear about what you are leaving and why.

Line 5 · The position of authority

A withdrawal made at the right moment, courteously and without drama, can leave relationships in good order. From a position of responsibility, the manner of stepping back matters as much as the decision itself.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

Far along the path of retreat, little is left to hold you, and the distance itself becomes a kind of ease. Where you have already let go, enjoy the freedom it gives without looking back to argue the case again.
