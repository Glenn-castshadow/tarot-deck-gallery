I Ching · Kinship

Hexagram 37: Jiā Rén 家人

Fire tended by wind

Trigrams
Fire below, Wind above
Keyword
Kinship

What hexagram 37 means

Wind feeds the Fire beneath it, each depending on the other to keep going. A household or close circle holds together through this same steady, mutual tending rather than through rules alone.

A question to sit with

What mutual tending, not just rules, keeps this circle together?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

Clear boundaries in a shared household keep later tension from taking hold. Where a circle is newly forming, agree early on how things are to be done.

Line 2 · The inner position

Tending to meals and daily needs at home, one steady person can hold much together. Such a household favours quiet care over ambitious projects that pull attention outward.

Line 3 · The threshold

Sharp words and raised voices at home can leave everyone raw. If you must lean one way, fairness that errs towards firmness serves a household better than letting everything slide, though warmth still needs room.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

An image of someone who brings real wealth to the home describes a person who enriches the shared life. Notice how your resources, practical or emotional, strengthen the people around you.

Line 5 · The position of authority

Influence at the heart of a household works best through affection rather than intimidation. From the place of authority, closeness and care do the work that rules alone cannot.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

Long service to a household earns respect when it rests on sincerity and self-examination. Look first at your own conduct, since dignity comes from honesty more than from rank.
