I Ching · Small measures

Hexagram 62: Xiǎo Guò 小過

Thunder passing a small mountain

Trigrams
Mountain below, Thunder above
Keyword
Small measures

What hexagram 62 means

Thunder moves above a modest Mountain, a small and careful action better suited to this moment than a grand gesture. Attending to small details now matters more than reaching for something larger than the moment calls for.

A question to sit with

What small, careful action fits this moment better than a grand gesture would?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

A young bird that takes flight before it is ready is the first image here. Stay close to the ground a while longer rather than soaring before you can sustain it.

Line 2 · The inner position

Being received by someone a step below the person you set out to reach describes an indirect route. Accepting a modest point of contact can be the right measure.

Line 3 · The threshold

Small precautions matter more than they seem, since what is not guarded against can come from behind. Take the extra care the moment asks for, even if it seems excessive.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Holding back from going beyond your role, you may still meet what you need without seeking it. As you enter the outer world, stay alert and let steady restraint, not force, set your pace.

Line 5 · The position of authority

Dense clouds from the west that do not yet bring rain describe potential not yet released. Look for capable help that has been out of sight, as a hunter reaches into a cave.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

The bird that flies past its mark and does not come to rest is the closing image. Overreaching when small steps were called for can leave you far from where you meant to be.
