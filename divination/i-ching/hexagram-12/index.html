I Ching · Separation

Hexagram 12: Pǐ 否

Earth and heaven apart

Trigrams
Earth below, Heaven above
Keyword
Separation

What hexagram 12 means

Earth settles low while Heaven rises further away, and the two no longer meet in the middle. When contact has thinned this much, small honest gestures matter more than large plans.

A question to sit with

What small, honest gesture could open contact that has gone quiet?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

A handful of grass pulled from the ground, roots still clinging together, pictures kindred spirits withdrawing as one. When the time is not open, stepping back with your allies can keep your aim intact. Who might you quietly align with while waiting?

Line 2 · The inner position

Putting up with the closed season, while others seek favour from those in power, is a real choice. Accepting a standstill without compromising yourself preserves your integrity. How can you stay true while the moment is closed?

Line 3 · The threshold

If a position has come before the substance to fill it, a private unease may be the first honest signal. Recognising that feeling can be a step towards change. What would it take to grow into such a place?

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Serving a purpose larger than your own interest can begin to turn a stalled time. Working alongside others towards that aim can open what was closed. Where might a wider purpose call on you?

Line 5 · The position of authority

At this seat of influence, a new gain is best tied firmly to something deep-rooted, like a mulberry that holds its ground. Remembering how fragile an opening can be helps steady it. What would keep this turn from slipping back?

Line 6 · The end, and excess

A blockage overturned by effort brings the relief of things moving again. After a long closed time, change can arrive when it is actively sought. What could you do now to help things clear?
