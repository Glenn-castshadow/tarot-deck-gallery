I Ching · Transience

Hexagram 56: Lǚ 旅

Fire flickering over mountain

Trigrams
Mountain below, Fire above
Keyword
Transience

What hexagram 56 means

Fire moves across the surface of a Mountain without settling into any one place on it, the way a traveler passes through unfamiliar ground. Adaptability and courtesy serve you well when you are somewhere you do not yet belong.

A question to sit with

Where am I still learning the customs of unfamiliar ground?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

Fussing over small change and minor grievances on the road can crowd out the journey itself. In unfamiliar ground, small-mindedness can close doors that dignity would keep open.

Line 2 · The inner position

The traveller reaches an inn with means to hand and a loyal companion found along the way. When you are settled in a modest, secure place, courtesy helps keep what support you have.

Line 3 · The threshold

The image is an inn catching fire and a companion lost through harsh treatment. A high-handed manner in someone else’s place can cost you the shelter you had.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Here the traveller finds a resting place and the means to defend it, yet the heart is not fully at ease. A foothold in the wider world may still feel provisional, and that unease is worth acknowledging.

Line 5 · The position of authority

A pheasant brought down at the cost of a single arrow is the image for this place of authority. A small outlay made with skill can earn recognition and a place among strangers.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

The image here is a bird whose nest catches fire, laughter turning to tears. If carelessness has cost the steady support you relied on, ask what a humbler bearing would restore.
