Hexagram 35: Jìn 晉

Fire rising over earth

Trigrams
Earth below, Fire above
Keyword
Advancement

What hexagram 35 means

Fire rises above the level ground of Earth, like a light climbing steadily into view. Visible advancement here comes from consistent effort meeting a moment ready to recognize it.

A question to sit with

What consistent effort is ready to be seen, if the moment allows it?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

Early progress may be met with doubt, and the trust of others has not yet arrived. Stay generous and even-tempered while it builds, since your direction does not need their approval to be sound.

Line 2 · The inner position

Sorrow can travel alongside real progress without cancelling it. Keep to the centre and stay open to care from an older, kindly figure, or from quarters you had not thought to look.

Line 3 · The threshold

Where many people share confidence in the direction, moving forward carries their agreement with it. Consensus can steady a step that would feel uncertain alone.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

An image of a hoarding squirrel describes advancing while clinging to more than you need. If progress at the edge of wider influence has turned acquisitive, ask what you are storing up and why.

Line 5 · The position of authority

The invitation is to stop weighing every gain and loss. Acting with a clear intention matters more here than tracking whether each step pays off.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

Advancing with lowered horns, the energy of progress turns back towards your own house. Use firmness to set your own affairs in order rather than to press on others.
