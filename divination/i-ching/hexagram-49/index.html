Hexagram 49: Gé 革

Fire meeting standing water

Trigrams
Fire below, Lake above
Keyword
Transformation

What hexagram 49 means

Fire and Lake stand in direct tension, heat against water, each capable of changing the other. Real transformation asks for timing and readiness, not just the wish that something old give way.

A question to sit with

What would genuine readiness for this change actually look like?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

An image of something bound tight in tough yellow hide suits a change that cannot move yet. The wish for change has stirred, but the ground for it has not yet been laid, so hold steady and let your reasons mature before acting on them.

Line 2 · The inner position

When the right day for change has arrived, preparation can give way to movement. A change that has been carefully considered can be carried forward with calm conviction.

Line 3 · The threshold

A proposal for change may need airing and testing repeatedly before others commit to it. Pushing ahead too hard can unsettle what you hope to improve, so listen for agreement before you move.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Here the change begins to reach the wider world, and doubts can ease once your purpose is plain to see. Where people can sense the sincerity behind a shift, they tend to accept a new arrangement more readily.

Line 5 · The position of authority

A tiger’s coat changing its pattern is the image for the one who leads a transformation from this place. When your aims are clear and openly held, others may trust the change before any result is visible.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

The leopard’s changing markings suggest a change that has already done its essential work. Now is a time to let the new pattern settle, since pressing for still more change could undo what has been gained.
