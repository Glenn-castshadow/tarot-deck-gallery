I Ching · Return

Hexagram 24: Fù 復

Thunder waking beneath earth

Trigrams
Thunder below, Earth above
Keyword
Return

What hexagram 24 means

Small and already in motion, a single Thunder line stirs beneath a wide field of Earth. A turning point often starts this quietly, well before it becomes visible from the outside.

A question to sit with

What quiet turning point might already be underway, even if unseen?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

Turning back after only a short distance is the gentlest kind of return. If you notice you have strayed a little, a quick correction costs very little. Early recognition keeps the path simple.

Line 2 · The inner position

A quiet, gracious return is made easier by good company. Consider whose example helps you come back to yourself. Humility makes the turning lighter.

Line 3 · The threshold

Returning again and again, after drifting away each time, is how some turnings go. Where a pattern of lapses and recoveries repeats, notice the effort each turning costs. Each return still counts, even if it is not the last.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Leaving the group’s path on your own suggests a return guided by your own sense of what is right. Consider where you might need to part from the crowd. Going your own way here is steadiness, not rebellion.

Line 5 · The position of authority

A wholehearted return means examining yourself honestly and without excuse. Consider where a sincere look inward would clarify what to change. Generosity towards yourself and others steadies the turning.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

The moment for returning has been missed, and confusion follows, like an army setting out without a clear road. If you feel lost, pause before pushing further in the same direction. Waiting until you can see the way is wiser than a forced advance.
