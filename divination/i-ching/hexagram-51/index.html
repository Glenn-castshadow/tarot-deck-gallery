I Ching · Alertness

Hexagram 51: Zhèn 震

Thunder answering its echo

Trigrams
Thunder below, Thunder above
Keyword
Alertness

What hexagram 51 means

Thunder repeats above Thunder, one shock followed closely by another. A sudden jolt can be unsettling and still leave you standing, provided you notice how quickly steadiness returns afterward.

A question to sit with

How quickly does my steadiness tend to return after a sudden jolt?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

Thunder arrives, and the first startled response can turn to relieved laughter. Letting the shock sharpen your attention, rather than unsettle you, is the beginning of steadiness.

Line 2 · The inner position

When a jolt threatens something you value, chasing after it may not be the wisest response. Seeking higher ground and letting time pass can let what matters find its way back.

Line 3 · The threshold

A shock that leaves you rattled can still become a spur to act more wisely. Is there a step you could take now that turns the jolt into movement?

Line 4 · Entering the outer

The image is thunder stuck in mud, its force dulled and slowed. Where a shock has left you bogged down, it may help to look for firmer footing before trying to push on.

Line 5 · The position of authority

Shocks may come and go around you in the position of authority. Staying centred lets you keep hold of the work in hand, whatever the noise nearby.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

Looking wildly about serves no one. If trouble has reached those nearby rather than you, this can be the moment to steady yourself and step back, even if others talk.
