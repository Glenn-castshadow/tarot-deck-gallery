I Ching · Abundance

Hexagram 14: Dà Yǒu 大有

Fire held high above

Trigrams
Heaven below, Fire above
Keyword
Abundance

What hexagram 14 means

Fire burns high above Heaven, visible from every direction, holding a great deal in clear view. What you have gathered asks to be used generously rather than simply guarded.

A question to sit with

What have I gathered that is meant to be shared, not just kept?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

When abundance is only beginning, a watchful simplicity protects it better than any display. What early care would suit what is starting to gather?

Line 2 · The inner position

A large wagon, able to carry a heavy load, pictures resources that can be put to real use. A wide capacity is best spent on moving things forward. Where could your means carry something worthwhile?

Line 3 · The threshold

Offering your means to a larger good gives abundance its fullest meaning. How could your resources contribute to something beyond yourself?

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Wealth that stays out of the spotlight draws less envy. As abundance becomes visible in a wider circle, a measured, unshowy manner can keep what you hold from becoming a burden. Consider where modesty might protect your position.

Line 5 · The position of authority

Here, influence rests on warmth that keeps its bearing. Openness can draw others in while a quiet composure keeps order. How might you be both warm and steady?

Line 6 · The end, and excess

Abundance blessed from above is the image. Having much while staying humble can let that fullness be welcomed rather than resented. What would it mean to hold your plenty lightly?
