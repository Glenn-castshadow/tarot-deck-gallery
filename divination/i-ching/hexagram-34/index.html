Hexagram 34: Dà Zhuàng 大壯

Thunder charging over heaven

Trigrams
Heaven below, Thunder above
Keyword
Vigor

What hexagram 34 means

Thunder rides above the full strength of Heaven, considerable power moving with real momentum. Strength this available still needs a clear aim, since force without direction tends to break what it meets.

A question to sit with

What clear aim would keep this strength from breaking what it meets?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

Strength gathered in the toes wants to rush ahead before the rest of the body is ready. Pushing forward on force alone tends to outrun your footing, so check your ground first.

Line 2 · The inner position

Kept within, power stays steady instead of showing off. Quiet consistency here does more than any display, and restraint is a good way to let your strength settle.

Line 3 · The threshold

A ram that charges a hedge ends up with its horns caught. When force meets resistance at a threshold, notice whether pressing harder is wisdom or simply habit, and consider holding your strength in reserve.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Where the hedge gives way, the path opens without a struggle, and strength moves like the axle of a heavy cart. Entering wider circles, steady forward motion works better than a sudden push.

Line 5 · The position of authority

Letting go of the ram’s stubbornness can be surprisingly easy once you see it clearly. From a place of authority, setting aside the need to prevail may cost far less than it might seem.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

Caught in the hedge, the ram can neither push on nor back out. If a situation has reached this kind of stalemate, admitting the difficulty openly is often what begins to loosen it.
