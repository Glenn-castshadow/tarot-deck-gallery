I Ching · Fullness

Hexagram 55: Fēng 豐

Thunder amplifying bright fire

Trigrams
Fire below, Thunder above
Keyword
Fullness

What hexagram 55 means

Thunder and Fire combine into something bright and considerable, a fullness near its own high point. Give full attention to what is genuinely present now, rather than assuming a peak like this holds itself in place.

A question to sit with

What deserves full attention while this fullness is actually here?

The six lines, bottom to top

A changing line is read on its own. These are original reflections written for this site, not a translation.

Line 1 · The beginning

In a time of abundance, you may meet someone whose strengths match your own. Spending time together as equals, for as long as the season lasts, is a fitting way to begin.

Line 2 · The inner position

The image is a screen so heavy that stars show at midday, brightness hidden behind it. Where pressing your case would only stir mistrust, steady sincerity may reach further than argument.

Line 3 · The threshold

Shade so deep that the smallest stars show in daylight marks the darkest point in this figure. If your capacity to act is curtailed, it may help to accept the limit and to work within it for now.

Line 4 · Entering the outer

Even behind a thick screen, the stars at midday can guide you towards a like-minded ally. Look for the person whose outlook matches yours and whose help you can return.

Line 5 · The position of authority

Bringing capable people forward lets brightness spread beyond one person. Welcoming talent and good counsel can turn fullness into something widely shared.

Line 6 · The end, and excess

A grand house screened from its neighbours is the closing image, and behind the gate there is no one to be seen. When fullness turns into withdrawal, consider whom you have shut out.
